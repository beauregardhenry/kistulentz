# Kistulentz 0.23.7 — Self-Edit Exercises That Target Your Weak Spots

Version 0.23.7 makes Self-Edit Exercises smarter about which passages it picks.

## Changed

- **Adaptive Self-Edit Exercises.** Practice now leans toward the categories your cross-project
  writing growth history shows you keep declining, instead of sampling uniformly at random from
  whatever a document happens to flag. A category you rarely need to decline still turns up
  sometimes -- just less often. A fresh install with no growth history yet sees the same uniform
  sampling as before, since there's nothing to weight against.

## Privacy and compatibility

All analysis remains local unless the author separately previews and approves an AI-backed
command. This universal application supports Apple silicon and Intel Macs running macOS
Sequoia 15 or later. Kistulentz is ad-hoc signed because Beau Henry does not yet have an Apple
Developer account.
