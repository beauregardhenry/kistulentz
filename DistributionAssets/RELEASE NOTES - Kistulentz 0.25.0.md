# Kistulentz 0.25.0 — A Lighter Parser, a Daily Recap, and Clearer Practice

Version 0.25.0 makes Kistulentz easier on your Mac and closes the loop in Your Writing Growth.

## Added

- **Today's recap.** Your Writing Growth now shows how clean today's writing was compared with
  *your own* usual day, and how many suggestions you applied or declined. It never judges how
  much you wrote, only the findings per 1,000 words, and it waits until you have three other
  scored days before comparing anything.
- **A "While you write" setting** for the optional English pack: *Responsive*, *Balanced*, or
  *Off*. Off skips parsing while you type; De-stink Review and reference analysis still use the
  pack when you run them.
- **Exercises say where they lean.** Self-Edit Exercises already favored the suggestions you
  decline most. They now tell you which ones, once there is enough history to say so.

## Changed

- **The language model unloads when idle.** The English pack's model now shuts itself down after
  three minutes without use and starts again when needed, instead of staying in memory all session.
- **Balanced is the new default.** Structural guidance now appears about two seconds after you
  pause instead of about 0.65 seconds. Pick *Responsive* in Settings to keep the old timing. The
  fast built-in checks are unchanged.

## Privacy and compatibility

All analysis remains local unless the author separately previews and approves an AI-backed
command. This universal application supports Apple silicon and Intel Macs running macOS
Sequoia 15 or later. Kistulentz is ad-hoc signed because Beau Henry does not yet have an Apple
Developer account.
