# The Lantern at Greywater

Mara reached the abandoned signal house before the storm closed the road. The brass lantern waited on its shelf, cold and unexpectedly clean. Someone had trimmed its wick since her last visit.

She opened the logbook. Beneath her father's final entry, a new line had appeared in blue pencil: *Light it at the turn of the tide.*

## The Returning Tide

At midnight, the harbor withdrew from the rocks. Mara struck a match. Across the dark water, three answering lights appeared where no islands stood.

She closed the shutter but kept the flame alive.
