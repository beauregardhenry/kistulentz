# A Field Notebook That Remains Useful

A field notebook becomes evidence only when another person can understand what was observed, where it happened, and how the observation was recorded. Decoration is optional. Context is not.

## Record the Conditions

Begin each session with the date, location, weather, and purpose. Distinguish direct observation from interpretation. When an estimate matters, write down the method used to make it.

## Preserve Uncertainty

Do not polish an uncertain observation into a confident fact. Mark illegible labels, approximate distances, and interrupted measurements while the limits are still fresh.

## Review Without Rewriting History

Later clarification belongs in a dated note. The original entry should remain legible so a reader can reconstruct both the observation and the correction.
